# Twitter (X) App Guide

## Navigation
- Bottom tab bar (left to right): Home (house icon), Search (magnifying glass), Communities/Grok, Notifications (bell), Messages/DM (envelope)
- "For You" / "Following" tabs at top of Home timeline to switch feed type
- "For You" = algorithmic feed, "Following" = chronological feed from accounts you follow
- Floating compose button: blue "+" icon at bottom-right, or tap the feather/quill icon in the tab bar

## Common Actions
- **Like**: Tap the heart icon below a tweet
- **Reply**: Tap the speech bubble icon below a tweet → type reply in the text field → tap "Reply" button
- **Retweet/Repost**: Tap the circular arrows icon → choose "Repost" or "Quote"
- **Bookmark**: Tap the share icon (arrow pointing up) → select "Bookmark"
- **Follow**: Tap the "Follow" button next to a user's name in tweets or profiles
- **View tweet detail**: Tap anywhere on the tweet body to open the tweet detail page with all replies
- **Open profile**: Tap the user's name or avatar to view their profile

## Search
- Tap the Search tab (magnifying glass) in the bottom bar
- Tap the search bar at the top and type your keyword
- Results have tabs: Top, Latest, People, Media, Lists
- "Top" shows most relevant results, "Latest" shows chronological results
- Trending topics appear below the search bar on the Search page

## Trending Topics
- Tap the Search tab in the bottom bar
- Scroll down to see "What's happening" / "Trends for you" section
- Each trend shows topic name, category, and tweet count
- Tap a trend to see tweets about that topic

## Follow Flow (for warm-up)
- Search a keyword (e.g., "mutualan", "cenblu", "follback") → select "Latest" tab
- Tap a tweet from the search results to open its detail page
- Follow the tweet creator: tap "Follow" button next to their name at the top of the tweet
- Scroll down to the replies section
- Follow users who replied: tap "Follow" next to each replier's name
- When you reach the bottom of replies, press system_button "back" to return to search results
- Tap the next tweet and repeat the process

## Composing a Tweet
- Tap the floating "+" button (bottom-right) or the feather icon in the tab bar
- Type your tweet in the text field
- Character limit is 280 (or more for premium accounts)
- Tap "Post" button (top-right) to publish

## Tips
- If a login screen appears, use type_secret with TWITTER_USERNAME and TWITTER_PASSWORD
- "Who to follow" suggestions can be dismissed with system_button "back"
- If a verification challenge appears (captcha, phone verification), do NOT attempt to solve it — call complete with success=false
- Sponsored/promoted tweets have a "Promoted" or "Ad" label — do not like or reply to these
- The bottom navigation bar contains five tabs (left to right): Home timeline, Search, Grok, Notifications, and DM. It auto-hides when you scroll down the timeline. If you need to access Search or any other tab while it's hidden, swipe UP on the timeline first to reveal the bar, then tap the desired tab.
- When scrolling, pause briefly between swipes to simulate reading
- If the app crashes or shows an error, close it with system_button "home" and reopen
